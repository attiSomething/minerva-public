Using Minerva to Manage Drafts

Every entry has one current draft. When you change it, the version before can go into the entry's Archive, so you can always go back. When a piece is done, mark that draft as final.

1. Open an entry and edit

Click any entry to open it. The text on the left is the current draft. [J] means it's still the original from a journal or scan; [C] means it's been composed or edited.

2. Choose how to save

  • Save (Overwrite draft) replaces the draft in place. Use it for small fixes.
  • Save (Archive current draft) moves the old version into Archive first, then saves your edit as the new [C] draft. Use it for real revisions.

3. Go back to an older version

The Old Drafts panel lists everything in Archive. View reads one; Restore brings it back as the current draft, after archiving the one you have now.

4. Mark the final draft

When the piece is finished, press Mark as final in the Share panel. Minerva keeps a copy in the entry's Final folder and shows a Final badge in the list. You can keep editing; the panel tells you when the current draft no longer matches the final. Marking a new final moves the old one into Archive with its date.

5. Get a second opinion

With AI-assisted organizing turned on, Ask Minerva can suggest a title, category and project, or proofread the draft. Nothing changes until you apply a suggestion and save.
