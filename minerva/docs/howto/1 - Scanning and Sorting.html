Scanning and Sorting for Minerva

Minerva can read your handwritten or typed pages, split them into separate pieces of writing, and file each one as an entry in your library. You check every word before anything is filed.

Before you start: reading pages needs AI-assisted scanning, turned on in Settings with your own provider and key. Proofreading and filing work without it.

1. Put your scans in the Sources folder

Give each notebook, letter bundle or stack of printouts its own folder inside Sources, with the files in a Scans folder inside it. PDFs and photos (.jpg, .png, .heic and others) both work.

2. Transcribe and build the Master

Open Scans, pick the source on the left, and choose the scans to read on the Transcribe tab. Each page is read once and kept, so you can stop and come back without paying for a page twice. Then press Build the Master: Minerva gathers everything into one file of numbered entries, joining a piece that runs onto the next page.

Typed pages are free. On Windows, Minerva first reads each page with the text recognition built into Windows, offline and at no cost. A page that comes back reading like typed or printed text is kept from that; handwriting goes on to your AI model. With AI turned off, the button reads Read typed pages and handwritten pages are left for later. You can turn this off in Settings under AI-assisted scanning.

3. Proofread each entry

The Proofread tab puts the entry's pages beside its text. Fix any misread words, read the notes about hard-to-read spots, and save. The chips above the page show which pages the entry is on; use Add page or Remove page if a piece starts or ends somewhere else.

4. File entries into your library

On File entries, tick the entries you're happy with, give each a title and category (or press Suggest titles and categories and check what comes back), then press File selected. Each one becomes its own folder with a [J] draft, and the table marks it Filed. If a poem or story runs across several entries, press join next under each entry number except the last, and they're filed as one piece.

5. Keep the drawings

Drawings and diagrams in your scans collect under Entries → Images. Check the suggested kind and name for each, tick the ones to keep, and press Save images. Their pages are copied into the source's Images folder, sorted by kind.

Good to know: filed entries remember their pages. Open one from Entries and its Scanned Pages panel shows the original handwriting next to your text.
