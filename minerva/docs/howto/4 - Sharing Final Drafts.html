Using Minerva to Share Final Drafts

When a piece is ready, export it to send or submit. When a collection is ready, Minerva puts the whole project together as a proof with covers, a title page and contents.

1. Mark your finals

Exports use an entry's final draft when it has one, and the current draft otherwise. See Using Minerva to Manage Drafts for marking finals.

2. Export one entry

In the entry's Share panel, choose a format (text, Word or PDF), which draft, and a template if you have one, then press Export. The file goes into the Exports folder in your library; a second export is saved alongside as "(2)" rather than replacing the first. Use Open or Show in folder to find it.

3. Use your own layout

In Settings, add a Word template with {{title}} and {{body}} where they belong (also {{category}}, {{date}} and {{author}}), or a PDF to use as letterhead. Pick it when you export.

4. Publish a project

Open the project, put its entries in order, and fill in the Publish panel: page size (6×9, 5×8, A5 and others), subtitle, author, copyright line, ISBN and any other details, plus front and back cover images. Then export a PDF proof, a Word manuscript or plain text.

Good to know: covers are scaled to fill the page, so use images in the same shape as your page size. Every export is a new file; your drafts are never changed by exporting.
