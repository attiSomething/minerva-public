Bringing In Old Drafts

Most writers arrive with drafts scattered across Word files, notes apps and old folders. Minerva can take them a piece at a time or all at once, shows you everything before it's created, and never changes the files you bring in.

1. One piece: New entry

Press New entry (on Home, or at the top of Entries). Give it a title, pick a category and, if you like, a project, then type or paste the text. Leave the title blank and Minerva uses the first line. It opens in the editor, ready to work on.

2. Many pieces in one paste

Paste a long document with the pieces separated by a line of ***, --- or ===, or by three blank lines, and tick Split into separate entries. The preview lists each piece with its title, so you can check the cut before anything is made. One or two blank lines are treated as a stanza break and kept.

3. Files: Import files

Press Import files and choose files or a whole folder. Minerva reads Word (.docx), OpenDocument (.odt), Rich Text, Markdown (including Obsidian notes and their tags), web pages, text files and PDFs that have real text in them. From Google Docs, use File > Download > Microsoft Word first. Each file becomes a row with its title, category, length and anything that couldn't be kept (tables, images, footnotes). Untick what you don't want and press Import.

4. From notes apps

Import files also opens whole exports, one entry per note, with labels and notebooks carried over as tags and each note's date kept:

  • Evernote: select a notebook, choose Export notebook, save as .enex, and import that file.
  • Google Keep: at takeout.google.com, choose only Keep and download the zip. Import the zip as it is; checklists come in as [ ] and [x] lines, and trashed notes are left out.
  • Notion: Export from a page's ••• menu, as Markdown & CSV with subpages included, and import the zip. Notion's long page IDs are dropped from the titles, and a subpage's parent becomes a tag.
  • Obsidian, Bear and other Markdown apps: import the files or folder, or, to keep bringing in new notes as you write them, set the folder up under Settings > Synced folders.
  • Apple Notes: Apple has no export for all your notes at once. A handful can be pasted into New entry (put *** between notes and tick Split). On a Mac, File > Export as PDF saves one note at a time, and those PDFs import as text. For many notes, a free exporter app from the Mac App Store can save them all as Markdown files to import. On Windows, open the notes at icloud.com and paste them in.

5. Handwritten notebooks and scans

Pages that are photos or scans go through the Scans page instead; see Scanning and Sorting for Minerva. A PDF with no text in it is pointed there too.

Good to know: importing copies the words into your library; the original files are only read. If a title is already in use, the new entry gets a number, like "Low Water (2)", so nothing is overwritten.
