Making a Standard Manuscript

Most magazines and contests ask for poems in standard manuscript format: your contact details at the top, the line count, the title in capitals and the poem in plain 12 pt type. Minerva lays it out for you.

1. Add your details once

In Settings > Manuscript details, fill in your name, address, email, phone and website. They're kept with Minerva's settings on this computer, never in your library.

2. Export the piece

Open the entry, and in its Share panel choose PDF or Word, then Standard manuscript as the layout, and press Export. If the entry has a final draft, you can choose it or the current one.

3. Check it

Press Open to look it over. Each piece starts on a new page; a stanza is kept together on one page when it fits, and later pages carry a running header with your surname, the title and the page number.

4. Send it

The file is in your library's Exports folder; Show in folder takes you there to attach it. Then record the submission (see Tracking Where a Poem Is Out).

Good to know: a magazine with its own rules always wins. If they want something different, a Word template of your own (Settings > Templates) can match it.
